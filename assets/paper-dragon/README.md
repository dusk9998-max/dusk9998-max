# Paper Dragon artwork

These layers were prepared with the built-in image generation tool from the
original Paper Dragon concept selected by the profile owner.

- dragon.png: transparent textured dragon layer; preserve alpha.
- background.jpg: warm paper, vermilion sun and ink-cloud background. The
  generated PNG background was encoded as JPEG at quality 88 for smaller SVGs.

The renderer embeds both assets in animated and still SVGs. It does not generate
new art or require an image service during nightly updates.

## Asset prompts

Dragon: extract the reference's elegant vermilion and ivory folded-paper dragon,
preserving its detailed head, mane, S-shaped body, gold seams, floral motifs and
whiskers. Remove the sun, clouds, background and frame to genuine transparency.
Do not simplify the dragon into vector segments or a circular ring.

Background: remove the entire dragon and reconstruct the warm rice-paper
surface. Preserve the muted red sun at upper left and gray-brown cloud curls
with gold outlines at lower left and upper right. Remove the dark frame. No
creature, text or new symbols.

## Growth forms

Hatchling, young, mature and elder PNGs were generated from dragon.png with the
built-in image generation tool and resized to a maximum 1024 pixels. Each keeps
the vermilion/ivory paper, floral motifs, gold seams and transparent background.
The hatchling has rounded features and horn buds; young has a slender body;
mature adds branched horns and mane; elder adds elaborate antlers and streamers.
These fixed assets are embedded locally; no image service runs in Actions.
