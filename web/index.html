How this portrait reads activity

Growth counts unique commits linked to this GitHub identity across readable branches. Shared commits in forks count once. Language proportions describe repository language bytes, weighted by authored commits; they do not measure lines personally written. The displayed mix is normalized across the four leading languages.

Weekly streaks combine active commit dates and GitHub contribution-calendar days, in Monday–Sunday UTC weeks. Calendar counts are not added to commit totals. Access covers repositories authorized by the configured tokens; some organization or collaborator repositories may be excluded. No private repository names, messages, or commit links are published.

Gold seals unlock at 25, 100, 300, 750 and 2,000 commits. Growth forms unlock at 50, 150, 500 and 1,500 commits. Streak accents build over 13 weeks and fade after a break; record streaks permanently enrich the pigments.
